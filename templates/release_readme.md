# Mighty Action Force: The Card Game (Print & Play)

An unofficial, non-commercial fan version of the [Mighty Action Force card game][card-game] from
[Poptropica][poptropica]'s [PoptropiCon island][poptropicon]. It's for two players, and you'll need two paperclips to
keep score.

[card-game]: https://poptropica.fandom.com/wiki/Mighty_Action_Force_Card_Game
[poptropica]: https://www.poptropica.com/
[poptropicon]: https://poptropica.fandom.com/wiki/PoptropiCon_Island

**Printing at home:** use the three PDFs in either `A4/` or `Letter/`, printed at 100% scale. `cards_backs.pdf` is
already mirrored for double-sided printing; flip on the long edge. The tuck box is on the last page of
`cards_fronts.pdf`, and the rules booklet folds from a single sheet with the steps printed on it.

**Ordering from a card printing service:** upload the images in `cards/bleed/` (822 × 1122 px, the usual poker-size
template with bleed), or `cards/trim/` if the service wants images without bleed. Each colour has its numbered card
fronts, one `back.png` shared by its character cards, and a separate back for its turn card. Order the scoreboard
twice if you want a spare, which makes 30 cards. The images only cover the cards, so you'll still want to print the
tuck box page and the rules booklet from one of the PDF folders.

This isn't for sale or any commercial use. Poptropica's material remains with its owners; everything else may be
shared and adapted for non-commercial use. See `LICENSE`.
